What's included

Everything a high-end full detail should be.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a full detail take?

A typical full detail takes 4–6 hours. Larger vehicles or cars with heavy sand, pet hair or stains may take longer.

What's the difference between mobile and drop-off?

It's the same full detail. Mobile ($260, Black Friday $130) means we come to your home or office in Honolulu. Drop-off ($200, Black Friday $100) means you bring your car to our Kaimuki location.

How long does the Black Friday 50% off last?

For mobile service, do you need water or power?

Requirements can vary by location. Ask when booking and we'll confirm what's needed for your spot.

How should I prepare my car?

Please remove personal items and valuables from the cabin, trunk and glovebox so every area can be cleaned thoroughly. Leave the rest to us.

What areas of Honolulu do you serve?

Mobile service is available in Honolulu. Ask when booking to confirm your neighborhood. Drop-off is in Kaimuki.

How do I pay?

Payment options are confirmed when you book. Ask when booking.

Is ceramic coating or paint correction included?

No. The full detail includes a paint sealant / wax for gloss and protection. Ceramic coating and paint correction are optional add-ons; ask when booking for a quote.

What if it rains?

Hawaii weather happens. If rain affects a mobile appointment, we'll work with you on timing. Ask when booking about rescheduling.
